# Information Security Governance

**Prepared by:** Edna Maburi  
**Type:** Governance design samples (illustrative / synthetic scenarios)  
**Audience:** Security leadership, GRC, and board-advisory stakeholders

> Illustrative scenarios for portfolio demonstration. Sector labels are synthetic and do not refer to a real client engagement.

## Summary

This project shows how I approach **enterprise security governance**: aligning cybersecurity with business goals, clarifying accountability, selecting frameworks that match organizational maturity, and producing artifacts leadership can use.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- Governance structure and reporting-line design
- RACI modelling for security activities
- Policy hierarchy and document-type discipline
- ISO 27001 / COBIT-oriented framework selection thinking
- Board-visible accountability and oversight mechanisms

## Guiding principles

- Governance enables the business by removing ambiguity
- Every security activity needs one accountable owner
- Board oversight is required for cyber risk to be treated as business risk
- Frameworks must fit maturity — not overwhelm it
- Metrics and reporting drive prioritization
