# Artifact: Security Steering Committee — Charter Outline

**Purpose:** Provide a reusable outline for a cross-functional Security Steering Committee (SSC).

## 1. Purpose

Ensure cybersecurity strategy, risk treatment, and major control investments remain aligned to business objectives and risk appetite.

## 2. Authority

- Recommends risk treatment priorities to executive leadership
- Approves / endorses security standards exceptions within delegated thresholds
- Escalates material residual risk to CEO / Board risk committee

## 3. Membership (illustrative)

- Chair: CISO
- Standing members: CIO/Head of IT, Head of Engineering, Legal/Compliance, Finance, Operations, Business unit representatives
- Optional: Internal Audit (independent observer)

## 4. Cadence

- Monthly operational meeting
- Quarterly risk deep-dive
- Ad-hoc sessions for material incidents or major change programs

## 5. Standing agenda

1. Risk posture changes and top residual risks  
2. Control exceptions and overdue remediations  
3. Major initiatives / architecture decisions with security impact  
4. Incident and near-miss themes  
5. Metrics: MTTD/MTTR, patch SLA, access review completion, phishing failure rate  

## 6. Inputs / outputs

**Inputs:** risk register updates, vuln remediation status, audit findings, incident reports  
**Outputs:** prioritized decisions, exception log, escalation pack for executives/board

## 7. Success measures

- Reduced aged critical vulnerabilities
- Clear ownership on open risks
- Faster exception cycle time with documented rationale
- Improved board-ready reporting quality
