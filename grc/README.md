### How to read these reports

Most write-ups follow a simple structure so reviewers can scan quickly:

- **Context**: industry + problem statement
- **Scope**: what was assessed / designed
- **Approach**: frameworks, reasoning, and method
- **Deliverables**: what artifacts were produced
- **Impact**: outcomes and measurable improvements (where possible)
