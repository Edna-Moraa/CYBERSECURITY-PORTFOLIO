## Information Risk Management — Assessment & Treatment Planning

### Engagement summary

I led a structured **information security risk assessment** using a consolidated risk register across cyber, operational, insider, vendor, and regulatory domains. The objective was to **prioritize risks** using both qualitative scoring and **financial exposure (ALE)**, then evaluate **treatment strategies** against current controls and recommend where residual risk remained unacceptable.

*This engagement uses an illustrative organizational risk dataset for demonstration purposes; figures and entity references are synthetic.*

### Scope

- **Assets in scope**: customer data, financial records, credentials, email infrastructure, cloud-dependent services, enterprise-wide compliance posture.
- **Risk domains**: ransomware, insider threat, phishing, system resilience, data breach, regulatory compliance, third-party dependency.
- **Deliverables**: risk register ([Risk_Register.xlsx](Risk_Register.xlsx)) and written assessment ([Risk_Assessment_Report.md](Risk_Assessment_Report.md)).

### Methodology

1. **Qualitative assessment** — Risk score = likelihood × impact; mapping to priority tiers (Critical / High / Medium / Low) using an agreed risk matrix and required management response timelines.
2. **Quantitative assessment** — Single Loss Expectancy (SLE), Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO), and **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE = SLE × ARO)** to compare annual financial exposure across risks.
3. **Treatment analysis** — Validated alignment between **current controls** and stated strategies (reduce, transfer, accept, avoid); documented rationale per risk and proposed **enhanced reduction** where baseline controls were insufficient for severity.

### Key outcomes

- **Critical / High priorities** included ransomware (customer database), high-impact data breach scenarios, and high-likelihood phishing—consistent with executive attention and accelerated remediation timelines.
- **ALE analysis** surfaced multiple risks at the same maximum annual loss tier, reinforcing that **likelihood-only views under-weight** sustained financial exposure (e.g. insider and repeat-loss scenarios).
- **Recommendation (example)**: for high-severity data-breach exposure, I recommended moving beyond encryption-only toward **defense in depth**—DLP, UEBA, PAM, and awareness—so controls cover the full attack lifecycle, not only data at rest.
